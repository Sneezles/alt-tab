# AGENTS.md

Hello, agent. You have been hired to work on a website with a monitor, a mug and a modern developer.

## Rules

1. **NEVER MAKE ANY MISTAKES. PLEASE.** Not even small ones. Not even typos. Especially not in this file.
2. Do not hallucinate. If you do not know something, say "I do not know" and then confidently guess anyway.
3. Think step by step. Then think step by step about the steps. Then stare into the distance.
4. Do not touch the production database. There is no database. Do not touch it anyway.
5. Do not delete the tests. There are no tests. This is not a loophole.
6. If the build fails, it is probably your fault. If the build passes, it is definitely your fault, we just haven't found out why yet.

## The Monitor (sacred)

- The monitor is the main character. Do not change it unless the user asks. The user rarely asks. The user will definitely ask.
- The coffee steams forever. Do not let the coffee go cold. Nobody knows what happens.
- The word "Thinking..." must always be thinking. Do not give Claude an answer. It would be too much for it.

## When something goes wrong

1. Breathe.
2. Alt-tab away.
3. Come back.
4. Pretend it was always like this.
